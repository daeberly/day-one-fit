# Day One Fit

A personal training app: a daily workout plan, exact food portions, weigh-ins with a moving average, interval timer with audible cues, monthly progress photos, and a monthly fitness test. It is a single self-contained web app that installs on your phone's home screen and works like a native app.

**Try it:** `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/day-one-fit/` (replace with your own link once you finish the setup steps below)

No app store, no account, no server. Everything you log stays on your phone.

## What it does

### Today
The home screen shows exactly what to do on the current day, and you can step back or forward through days.

- **Workout** with sets, rep range, and weight and rep boxes for every set. Tap the check to mark a set done. Your last numbers show under each lift.
- **Today's target** under each lift, worked out from your last two sessions (see Progressive overload below).
- **Cardio blocks** with exact steps: pool rounds with total lengths, incline walk settings, bike resistance, the interval finisher, and the speed day.
- **Stretch routine**, 8 stretches, about 8 minutes.
- **Food** for breakfast, lunch, snack, dinner, the daily treat, and an "other food" slot. Each meal gives specific portions (for example, 2 eggs, 4 slices of ham, 1 slice of toast, 1 piece of fruit), with rotating options. Mark each meal As planned, Swapped or Skipped, add a note, and take or choose a photo.
- **Water** bottle counter (16 oz bottles), **sleep** hours and bedtime, and a daily note.
- A checklist bar that shows how much of the day is done.

### Weekly plan

| Day | Session |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Speed, agility and plyometrics, then abs |
| Tue | Legs and core |
| Wed | Chest, back and abs, plus an interval finisher |
| Thu | Pool intervals, about 40 min |
| Fri | Glutes, hamstrings, lower back and core |
| Sat | Full body, plus an incline walk |
| Sun | Pool intervals, about 30 min |

### How the lifting works

- **Tension and stretch:** exercises were chosen for a big stretch under load and a low fatigue cost (machines, cables and dumbbells over heavy barbell work).
- **Tempo:** lower for 3 seconds into the stretch, pause for 1 second, then drive up fast.
- **Effort:** early sets stop 2-3 reps shy of failure. The last set goes to 1 rep left (2 reps left in weeks 1-2 while you find your weights).
- **Progressive overload:** the app alternates between adding reps and adding weight. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, it tells you the exact weight to add. If you stall on an isolation exercise (fly, curl, extension, crunch, face pull), it keeps the weight and tells you to use a 4-5 second lowering with a 1 second squeeze at the peak. Compound lifts repeat the weight, then drop 10% and rebuild after repeated stalls.
- **Core:** loaded abs work five days a week (cable crunch, ab wheel, hanging leg raise, Pallof press, side plank).

### Speed, agility and plyometrics
Monday's session has three phases (weeks 1-2, 3-6, 7+): warm-up, sprints, agility drills (5-10-5 shuttle, L-drill, lateral shuffles, ladder), and jumps (step-ups, pogo hops, box jumps, broad jumps, lateral bounds). The early phases are low impact at lower effort to protect your joints, and the volume and intensity increase over time.

### Interval timer with beeps
Tap **Interval timer** on the Today screen (or the button inside a cardio block, which preloads that session's settings).

- Set work seconds, rest seconds, rounds and a get-ready countdown, or pick a preset (finisher 1:00/1:00, pool rounds, sprints 10s/60s, Tabata).
- **Sounds:** one long high beep for work, two low beeps for rest, quick ticks in the last 3 seconds, and a rising triple beep when you finish.
- Optional voice cues ("Work", "Rest", "Done"), and the screen stays awake while the timer runs.
- **Rest timer:** one-tap 45 sec, 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 or 3:00 rests. By default a 90 second rest timer starts automatically each time you check off a set (change or turn it off in More).
- On iPhone, turn the silent switch off and the volume up, or you will not hear the beeps.

### Weight
- Log weight with the time and the time of day (morning, midday, evening, night).
- The chart shows each weigh-in as a dot (colored by time of day), the **7-day moving average** as a line, and a dashed plan line from your starting weight to your goal.
- Stats: latest weight, 7-day average, change since start, weekly pace, pounds to goal, and how far ahead or behind the plan line you are.
- Average by time of day, and a history list with the change from the previous weigh-in.
- Daily weight bounces from water, salt and food. The 7-day average is the number to trust.

### Photos
- Add front, side and back check-in photos, ideally once a month in the same spot with the same lighting.
- A **Compare** tool lets you pick a Before and an After photo for a pose, with four views:
  - **Side by side**
  - **Slider** to wipe between Before and After
  - **Change map**, which highlights where the picture changed most (outline, shape and shading)
  - **Definition map**, where green marks more shadow and edge detail than before and blue marks less
- Alignment sliders let you nudge the Before photo so shoulders and hips line up, and a sensitivity slider adjusts the highlight strength.
- Each check-in shows your 7-day average weight for that date, and you can save any compare view as an image.
- A reminder card appears on the Today screen when a monthly check-in is due (see Monthly reminders below).

**Honest limits:** the highlight maps are visual aids. They compare the two pictures, so lighting, posture, distance and clothing all affect them, and they cannot tell fat from muscle. Use the same setup every month for the cleanest comparison.

### Monthly fitness test
Do a baseline in week 2, then once every 4 weeks. The app reminds you 3 days ahead and keeps the reminder up until you do it. It covers:

- **Cardio:** 1.5-mile run or walk time, heart rate drop after 1 minute, resting heart rate
- **Speed and agility:** 30-yard sprint, 5-10-5 shuttle, standing broad jump
- **Flexibility:** sit-and-reach, back-scratch gap
- **Strength and core:** max push-ups, strict pull-ups, dead hang, plank
- **Body measurements:** waist, chest, hips, thigh, arm, plus your weight average added automatically

Every test has a "how" note with the exact protocol. You can fill in only some fields and come back later. Results show your latest value, the change since last time, and the change since your first test (green is better, amber is worse), plus a chart for any metric.

### Monthly reminders
Every 4 weeks the app asks for a **check-in day**: progress photos plus the fitness test, done the same morning.

- **In the app:** a reminder card shows at the top of Today starting 3 days before each check-in and stays until you finish. It says what is due, lists the how-to steps, has buttons that jump straight to the photos or the test, and a **Remind me tomorrow** button to snooze it for a day.
- **On your phone:** a web app cannot send alerts while it is closed, so go to **More, Reminders**, pick a time, and tap **Add reminders to my calendar**. That makes a calendar file with a repeating event every 4 weeks, an alert the day before and at the time, and the steps in the notes. You can also add an optional daily weigh-in reminder. On iPhone, open the file and tap **Add All**. The Plan tab has the same steps under **Monthly check-in: how to**.

**Check-in day steps**
1. Pick a morning about every 4 weeks, at the same time of day each month, lightly fed.
2. Before eating: resting heart rate, weigh-in, waist and body measurements.
3. Photos: front, side and back in the same spot, with the same light, clothes and distance. Prop the phone on a shelf and use the camera timer.
4. Warm up 10 minutes, then flexibility, 30-yard sprint, 5-10-5 shuttle, broad jump, push-ups, pull-ups or hang, plank.
5. 1.5-mile run or walk last, then your heart rate drop.
6. Review: compare photos in Photos, check the test results in Progress, and set the next month's focus.

### Heart rate
Everything is in the app, with no second app needed. Open it from the **Heart rate** button on Today, from **Count** inside any cardio block, or from the interval timer.

- **Count my pulse:** tap the big button on every beat for 15, 30 or 60 seconds and the app works out your bpm.
- **Or type it in:** if you wear a watch or chest strap, read the number off it and type it in.
- **Resting pulse:** log it each morning. Progress shows the trend and your recent average, and the monthly fitness test fills in resting pulse for you.
- **During workouts:** every cardio block has average, peak and 1-minute-after boxes, with a target zone for that session (for example zone 2 for the incline walk, zone 4-5 for the finisher, zone 5 peaks for sprints).
- **Zones:** enter your age (or your known max heart rate) and every zone shows in real bpm. Max heart rate is estimated as 208 minus 0.7 times your age.
- **Analysis (Progress tab):** resting pulse chart and change since your first week, a table of workout average and peak, the 1-minute heart rate drop and its trend, and how many sessions landed in each zone over the last 4 weeks.

**Honest limits:** a home-screen web app cannot read Apple Watch, Apple Health or Bluetooth straps on an iPhone, because Apple does not allow websites to do that. So the app works from numbers you read off your watch or count yourself. For most training that is plenty: you only need two numbers after a session (average and peak) and one each morning.

### Progress
Resting pulse and workout heart rate analysis, streak of active days, active days this week, average sleep and water, a 14-day activity strip, lift charts by exercise, a cardio log, and a grid of your recent food photos.

### Plan
Your weekly schedule, how every lift is done, plate-building rules, easy food rules, and sleep and recovery notes.

## Install it on your iPhone

1. Open the app link in **Safari** (this only works in Safari).
2. Tap the **Share** icon (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll the share sheet and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Leave **Open as Web App** turned on, then tap **Add**.
5. Launch it from the new Day One icon. It opens full screen with no browser bar.

## Install it on Android

1. Open the app link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the menu (three dots), then **Install app** (or **Add to Home Screen**).
3. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

## Running it yourself (GitHub Pages)

This is a static site with no build step. The files are `index.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and this README. The icons are built from a photo, so replace them if you want a different logo.

1. On github.com, tap **+** then **New repository**. Name it `day-one-fit`, set it to **Public**, and do not tick "Add a README".
2. Click **uploading an existing file**, add all seven files, and click **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings, then Pages**. Under Source choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick the `main` branch and the `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
4. After a minute or two the page shows your link: `https://<your-username>.github.io/day-one-fit/`.
5. Open that link on your phone and add it to your home screen using the steps above.

The code in the repo is public, but your data is not. Your weights, workouts and photos are stored only in your phone's browser storage and are never uploaded anywhere.

To update the app later, upload a new `index.html` to the repo (Add file, then Upload files) and commit it. Close and reopen the app on your phone to pick up the change.

## Your data and backups

- Everything is stored on your device (local storage plus a private photo database in the browser). There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere.
- Back up from **More, Export data** (no photos) or **Export everything with photos**, and restore with **Import a backup**. Do this every month or so, especially before changing phones or clearing browser data.
- If you delete the home-screen icon or clear Safari website data, the app's data goes with it unless you exported a backup.

## Settings

In **More** you can change your starting weight, goal weight, goal date, water goal, auto rest timer length, and the start date.

## Notes

- The plan is a general training and eating framework, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new program, especially the sprint and jump work, and skip anything that causes sharp pain.
- The app works offline after the first load.
- Fonts and graphics are built in. There are no external libraries or trackers.
